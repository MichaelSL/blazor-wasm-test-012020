.NET Regex Tester

Test .NET (C#) regular expressions online. Patterns run on the real System.Text.RegularExpressions engine, compiled to WebAssembly, so all your test data stays in your browser.

  • Highlighted matches, a match table and capture groups (including named groups)
  • All RegexOptions: IgnoreCase, Multiline, Singleline, ExplicitCapture, IgnorePatternWhitespace, RightToLeft, CultureInvariant, ECMAScript, NonBacktracking and AnyNewLine
  • Regex.Split and Regex.Replace with substitutions such as $1 and ${name}
  • Sample patterns, and export of matches as JSON, CSV or text
